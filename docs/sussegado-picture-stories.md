# Sussegado — Picture Stories & Illustration Prompts

Brief on purpose — a few seconds per frame, the illustration carries the rest.
Every prompt is fully self-contained: copy the whole block straight into
ChatGPT, Gemini, or your image tool of choice.

## PART 1 — Introduction (5 frames)

### Frame 1 — the thesis
**EN:** Food is never just a recipe. It's shaped by the place it grows in,
the empires that pass through it, and the economics of who could afford
what.
**ES:** La comida nunca es solo una receta. La moldea el lugar donde crece,
los imperios que pasaron por ella, y la economía de quién podía permitirse
qué.

No text, no logos, no lettering.

## STORY PAGE — "What Sussegado Means" (single frame)

This is the illustration for the story page itself (the one that defines the
word), not part of the 5-frame intro sequence. It deliberately echoes the
logo's balcão icon.

**Text on this page (unchanged from the booklet):**
"It's a word my family back in Goa still uses — for a certain kind of
afternoon, a certain kind of meal. It means unhurried. Settled. Content, in
a quiet way that doesn't need to prove anything."

screen instead of an actual Goan oyster-shell window; not Goan enough):**

Simple, uncluttered composition. No text, no logos, no lettering.

The house is whitewashed with ochre-yellow trim and a sloping red-tiled roof.
